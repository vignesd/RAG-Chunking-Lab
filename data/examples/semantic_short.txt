Artificial intelligence refers to computer systems capable of performing tasks that typically require human intelligence, such as understanding language, recognizing images, and making decisions. Machine learning, a subset of AI, allows systems to improve their performance on a task by learning patterns from data rather than following explicitly programmed rules. Deep learning, a further subset, uses layered neural networks to automatically discover complex patterns in large datasets, powering applications like voice assistants, recommendation engines, and image recognition systems.
The human digestive system breaks down food into nutrients the body can absorb and use for energy, growth, and cell repair. Digestion begins in the mouth, where chewing and saliva start breaking down food, continues in the stomach with acid and enzymes, and finishes in the small intestine, where most nutrient absorption occurs. The large intestine absorbs water and forms waste for elimination. This innovation contributed to rising literacy rates, the spread of new ideas during the Renaissance and Reformation, and the standardization of languages across regions. Before the printing press, books were rare and expensive, limiting access to knowledge mostly to the wealthy and religious institutions.
